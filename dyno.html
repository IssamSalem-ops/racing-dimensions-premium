ProcessHOW A SESSION RUNS

Four stages, one afternoon.

Most tunes are completed in a day. Bring the car with at least a quarter tank of the fuel you actually run.

01

Strap down & baseline

The car is secured to the rollers and we take a baseline run — the honest starting number, before anything changes.

02

Log & inspect

Wideband AFR, boost, knock and intake temps are logged live. We look for the problems before we chase the power.

03

Map on the rollers

Fuel, ignition and boost written run by run for your fuel and Jordan's climate — not a file bought off the internet.

04

Verify & hand over

Final pulls, a printed HP/torque graph and the logs. You leave knowing exactly what the car makes.
