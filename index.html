01 / CapabilitiesWHAT WE DO

Everything that makes power — under one roof.

Most shops sell you a part. We take responsibility for the whole outcome: the spec, the fitment, the map, and the number on the graph at the end of it.

01 Dyno Center Load-bearing rollers, wideband logging and a printed power graph. The only honest way to prove a build. 02 Custom ECU Mapping Standalone, piggyback and factory-ECU remaps written on the rollers for your fuel, your climate, your goals. 03 Performance Parts 20 categories across 34 authorised brands — turbochargers, internals, suspension, brakes, wheels, racewear. 04 Engine Building Forged bottom ends, head work and blueprinting for engines that survive the power they make. 05 Fabrication & Fitment Exhaust systems, intakes, intercooling and hard lines — fabricated and fitted in-house. 06 Diagnostics Full scan, compression and health check before you spend a dinar on power.
